Christ Community Church

For over 100 years, CCC has been called to make disciples, reach the lost in Omaha, and send the gospel around the world. Reach One More gave that calling fresh expression for a new generation.

Reach One More launched in fall 2023 and ran through December 2024. This page explains what it was and why it still shapes who we are.

A Letter from Minister Mark Ashton

Why Reach One More?

Coming out of COVID-19, our elders spent a season in prayer asking God what was next for Christ Community Church. We held more than thirty focus groups and two leadership retreats to listen well.

What emerged was a clear picture of CCC's fingerprint: a central place in the Omaha Metro, a deep care for people who have never heard of Jesus, a culture of generosity, a willingness to take faith-filled risks, a holy dissatisfaction with the status quo, strong care and counseling ministries, and a passion to reach one more.

With 102 years of ministry behind us, a strong campus, and no debt, the easy choice would have been to coast. Our elders chose to press forward. Reach One More is not a property campaign. It is an evangelistic initiative to reach one more person here, there, and everywhere.

I am grateful for everyone who joined us in this work.

Minister Mark Ashton

Lead Minister

FAQ

Questions

What was Reach One More?

A church-wide initiative launched in October 2023 that called every person at CCC to pray for, love, and share Jesus with one person in their life, and to give generously toward reaching people here in Omaha, there in hard places, and everywhere among the least-reached nations.

How long did the financial commitment run?

Thirteen months, from the First Big Give on December 3, 2023 through December 2024. Gifts were given above regular giving into one fund supporting core ministry, local biblical justice, global missions, and campus upgrades.

Is reaching one more still something we do?

Yes. The giving campaign had an end date. Reaching one more does not. It is a lifelong commitment for every follower of Jesus, and it remains part of CCC's DNA.

What if I don't know any non-Christians?

Start by praying that God would bring someone into your life who is open to friendship, and that he would make you a fisher of people.

What if I don't know how to share my faith?

CCC offers evangelism and Seeker Small Group training. Find current options at cccomaha.info under Classes & Events.

I'm new to CCC. Where do I start?

We would love for you to make CCC your church home. Take CCC 101 at cccomaha.info, or reach out to our team at cccomaha.org/staff.
